Make Orka feel like your own by customizing your workspace branding.

## Branding options

- **Logo** — Upload your company logo
- **Colors** — Choose your brand colors
- **Custom domain** — Use your own domain for the client portal
- **Email templates** — Customize invoice and proposal emails

## How to update branding

1. Go to **Workspace Settings**
2. Click **Branding**
3. Upload your logo
4. Select your brand colors
5. Save changes

> **Business Tip:** Consistent branding across proposals, invoices, and the client portal builds professionalism and trust.
