Make Orka match your brand with custom themes.

## Theme options

- **Light mode** — Clean, bright interface
- **Dark mode** — Easy on the eyes
- **Brand colors** — Match your company colors

## How to set a theme

1. Go to **Workspace Settings**
2. Click **Themes**
3. Choose light or dark mode
4. Customize accent colors

## Brand consistency

- Your theme applies to client-facing pages
- Proposals and invoices reflect your brand
- Client portal matches your workspace

> **Business Tip:** Use your brand colors consistently across Orka. It makes every client interaction look professional.
