Connect Orka with the tools you already use.

## Available integrations

- **Slack** — Get notifications in Slack
- **Google Calendar** — Sync project deadlines
- **Zapier** — Connect to 5000+ apps
- **Stripe** — Accept card payments

## How to connect

1. Go to **Workspace Settings**
2. Click **Integrations**
3. Find the tool you want to connect
4. Click **Connect**
5. Authorize the connection

## Managing integrations

- Toggle integrations on/off
- Reconfigure settings
- Disconnect when needed

> **Business Tip:** Start with one or two integrations. Add more as your workflow evolves.
