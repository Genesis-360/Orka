Get intelligent suggestions throughout Orka to work faster and smarter.

## Where suggestions appear

- **Proposals** — Suggested scope and pricing
- **Invoices** — Auto-filled details and terms
- **Projects** — Recommended milestones
- **Clients** — Follow-up reminders

## How suggestions work

- AI learns from your past projects
- Suggestions improve over time
- You always accept or modify suggestions

## Enabling smart suggestions

1. Go to **Workspace Settings**
2. Click **AI Features**
3. Toggle **Smart Suggestions**

> **Business Tip:** The more you use Orka, the better AI suggestions become. It learns your patterns and preferences.
