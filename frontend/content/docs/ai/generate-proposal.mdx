Orka's AI proposal generator helps you create professional proposals that win clients.

## How it works

1. Click **AI** in the sidebar
2. Select **Generate Proposal**
3. Enter the project details
4. Review the AI-generated proposal
5. Send it to your client

## What the AI considers

The proposal generator analyzes:

- Your industry and services
- The client's needs
- Market rates for similar work
- Your past project history
- Proposed timeline and deliverables

> **Business Tip:** Customize the AI-generated proposal with your unique value proposition. The AI gives you a strong starting point — your personal touch makes it win.

## Proposal structure

A winning proposal includes:

- **Executive summary** — What you'll deliver and why
- **Scope of work** — Detailed deliverables
- **Timeline** — Key milestones and dates
- **Pricing** — Clear cost breakdown
- **Terms** — Payment and revision terms
- **About you** — Your credentials and experience

## Sending proposals

Once your proposal is ready:

1. Review and customize the content
2. Add your branding
3. Send via email or shareable link
4. Track when the client views it
5. Get notified on acceptance

> **Business Tip:** Follow up within 24 hours if a proposal hasn't been responded to. A quick check-in shows professionalism.
