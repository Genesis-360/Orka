Automate invoice creation with AI that understands your projects.

## How it works

1. Complete a milestone
2. AI drafts an invoice automatically
3. Review the details
4. Send to client

## What AI handles

- Line items from project scope
- Correct pricing and quantities
- Payment terms and due dates
- Tax calculations

## Customizing invoices

- Edit any field before sending
- Add custom line items
- Choose invoice template

> **Business Tip:** Review AI-generated invoices quickly. They save time on repetitive data entry while you maintain control.
