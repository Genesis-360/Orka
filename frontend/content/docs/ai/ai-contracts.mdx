Let AI draft contracts based on your project details and scope.

## How it works

1. Open a project
2. Click Generate Contract
3. AI analyzes project scope and terms
4. Review and customize the draft
5. Send to client for signature

## What AI includes

- Scope of work
- Payment terms
- Revision policy
- Timeline and milestones

## Customizing contracts

- Edit any section before sending
- Save custom clauses for reuse
- Choose from contract templates

> Business Tip: Always review AI-generated contracts before sending. They're a great starting point, but you know your client best.
