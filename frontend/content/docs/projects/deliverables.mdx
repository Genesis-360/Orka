Define what needs to be delivered for each milestone and track completion.

## What are deliverables

Deliverables are the specific outputs or results that complete a milestone. They give you and your client a clear definition of "done."

## How to add deliverables

1. Open a project
2. Go to **Milestones**
3. Select a milestone
4. Click **Add Deliverable**
5. Describe the deliverable and set a due date

## Tracking progress

- Deliverables show as **pending** until marked complete
- Clients can approve individual deliverables
- Completed deliverables unlock milestone payment

> **Business Tip:** Break large milestones into smaller deliverables. It makes progress visible and reduces disputes.
