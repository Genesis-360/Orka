Save time by creating reusable project templates.

## How to create a template

1. Open an existing project
2. Click Save as Template
3. Name your template
4. Choose what to include (milestones, deliverables, settings)

## Using a template

1. Click New Project
2. Select From Template
3. Choose your template
4. Customize as needed

## What templates include

- Project structure and milestones
- Default deliverables
- Team assignments
- Payment terms

> Business Tip: Create templates for your most common project types. It standardizes your process and saves hours of setup.
