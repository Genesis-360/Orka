## General

What is Orka?
Orka is an AI-powered financial operating system for service businesses. It helps you manage proposals, contracts, projects, invoices, and payments in one place.

Who is Orka for?
Orka is built for freelancers, agencies, consultants, studios, and modern service businesses.

Is Orka free to use?
Yes. The Starter plan is free forever and includes 1 workspace, up to 3 projects, and on-chain escrow in USDC. Studio adds more projects, automation, and analytics for $29/month (or $290/year — 2 months free). Enterprise is custom-priced for agencies with SSO, dedicated chain infrastructure, and white-glove onboarding.

## Payments

What payment methods are supported?
Orka supports Stellar wallet transfers, bank transfers, and credit cards (where available).

Are there transaction fees?
Stellar transactions have minimal network fees. Orka does not charge additional transaction fees.

How long do payments take?
Stellar payments are typically confirmed within 3-5 seconds. Bank transfers may take 1-3 business days.

## Escrow

How does escrow work?
Funds are deposited into escrow when a project starts. They're released to your wallet when milestones are approved by the client.

What if there's a dispute?
Orka provides a dispute resolution process. Both parties can submit evidence, and the platform helps facilitate a fair resolution.

## Wallet

Which wallets are supported?
Orka currently supports Freighter for Stellar wallet connections.

Is my wallet secure?
Orka never has access to your private keys. All transactions are approved by you in your wallet extension.
