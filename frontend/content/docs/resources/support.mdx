Get help when you need it.

## Support channels

- **Help Center** — Search articles and guides
- **Email** — support@orka.io
- **Live Chat** — Available during business hours
- **Community** — Ask the community

## Response times

- **Critical issues** — Within 4 hours
- **General questions** — Within 24 hours
- **Feature requests** — Reviewed weekly

## Before contacting support

1. Check the help center
2. Search existing community posts
3. Gather relevant details (screenshots, steps to reproduce)

> **Business Tip:** The help center has answers to most common questions. It's the fastest way to get unstuck.
