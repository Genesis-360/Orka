Collaborate with your team by inviting them to your Orka workspace.

## How to invite members

1. Go to **Team** in your workspace
2. Click **Invite Members**
3. Enter their email address
4. Select their role
5. Click **Send Invite**

## Roles

| Role | Permissions |
|------|------------|
| Admin | Full access to all workspace settings, billing, and team management |
| Member | Can manage projects, clients, invoices, and team activity |
| Viewer | Read-only access to projects and reports |

> **Business Tip:** Start with fewer admins. You can always promote team members later as your trust grows.

## What happens after inviting

Your team member will receive an email invitation. Once they accept:

- They'll see your workspace in their Orka dashboard
- They can start collaborating on projects
- Their activity will appear in the activity feed

## Managing team members

As an admin, you can:

- Change member roles
- Remove members from the workspace
- View team activity and permissions
- Set up approval workflows

> **Business Tip:** Set up approval workflows early. This ensures quality control as your team grows.
