Connect your Stellar wallet to start sending and receiving payments through Orka.

## Why connect a wallet?

Orka uses the Stellar network for fast, low-cost cross-border payments. Connecting your wallet enables:

- Receiving payments from clients
- Funding escrow for milestones
- Sending payments to team members
- Cross-border transactions

## 1. Install Freighter

Freighter is a browser extension for interacting with the Stellar network.

- Visit the Freighter website
- Install the extension for your browser
- Create or import a Stellar account

## 2. Connect to Orka

Once Freighter is installed:

1. Go to your workspace settings
2. Click "Connect Wallet"
3. Approve the connection in Freighter
4. Your wallet address will appear in Orka

> **Business Tip:** Use a dedicated wallet for your business finances. This makes accounting and tax reporting much easier.

## 3. Choose your currency

Select the default currency for your workspace:

- **USD** — US Dollar
- **EUR** — Euro
- **GBP** — British Pound
- **XLM** — Stellar Lumens

## Security

Your wallet keys never leave Freighter. Orka can only request transactions — you always approve them in the extension.

> **Business Tip:** Never share your Freighter password or recovery phrase with anyone, including Orka support.
