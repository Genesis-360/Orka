Welcome to Orka — the AI-powered financial operating system built for freelancers, agencies, consultants, studios, and modern service businesses.

## What is Orka?

Orka helps you run your entire service business from one place. Instead of juggling multiple tools for proposals, contracts, projects, invoices, and payments, Orka brings everything together.

**With Orka you can:**

- Win clients with AI-generated proposals
- Manage projects with milestones and deliverables
- Handle contracts and collect signatures
- Create invoices and receive payments
- Manage escrow for secure transactions
- Collaborate with your team
- Automate repetitive work using AI

## Why service businesses choose Orka

Traditional project management tools were built for software teams. Orka was built specifically for service businesses — freelancers who need to get paid, agencies managing multiple clients, and consultants juggling projects.

> **Business Tip:** Start by setting up your workspace completely. A strong foundation saves time later.

## Quick overview

Here's what you'll accomplish in your first few minutes with Orka:

1. **Create your workspace** — Set up your business hub
2. **Connect your wallet** — Enable payments on Stellar
3. **Invite your team** — Bring collaborators aboard
4. **Add your first client** — Start building relationships
5. **Create a project** — Define scope and milestones
6. **Get paid** — Send invoices and receive payment
