## What is Orka?

Orka helps you run your entire service business from one place. Instead of juggling multiple tools for proposals, contracts, projects, invoices, and payments, Orka brings everything together.

## Why service businesses choose Orka

Traditional project management tools were built for software teams. Orka was built specifically for service businesses — freelancers who need to get paid, agencies managing multiple clients, and consultants juggling projects.

<BusinessTip>
Start by setting up your workspace completely. A strong foundation saves time later.
</BusinessTip>
