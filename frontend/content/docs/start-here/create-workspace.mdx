Your workspace is your business hub in Orka. It contains your projects, clients, invoices, team, and settings.

## 1. Click on "Create Workspace"

After signing up, click on the "Create Workspace" button on the onboarding screen.

## 2. Add your workspace details

Enter your business name, choose your industry, and select your preferred currency.

- Workspace Name — Your business or team name
- Industry — Select the industry that best describes your work
- Currency — The default currency for invoices and payments

> Business Tip: Choose a currency that matches where most of your clients are based. You can always change this later.

## 3. Invite your team (optional)

Invite your team members to collaborate and manage your projects together.

Enter their email addresses and assign roles:

- Admin — Full access to all workspace settings
- Member — Can manage projects, clients, and invoices
- Viewer — Read-only access to projects and reports

## 4. You're all set!

Your workspace is ready. You can now add clients, create projects, and start getting paid.

## What to do next

- [Connect your wallet](/docs/start-here/connect-wallet) to enable payments
- [Add your first client](/docs/clients/add-client) to start building relationships

> Business Tip: Complete your workspace setup before adding clients. A professional workspace builds trust.
