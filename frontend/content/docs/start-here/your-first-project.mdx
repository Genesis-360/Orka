Let's walk through creating your first project in Orka from start to finish.

## Step 1: Create the project

1. Click Projects in the sidebar
2. Click Create Project
3. Enter the project name
4. Select a client (or create one)
5. Set the project budget and timeline

## Step 2: Define milestones

Break your project into milestones:

- Milestone 1 — Initial deliverable
- Milestone 2 — Review and revisions
- Milestone 3 — Final delivery

Each milestone should have:
- A clear description
- A due date
- An amount for payment

> Business Tip: Define deliverables before starting work. This prevents scope creep and keeps projects on track.

## Step 3: Set up escrow

Fund escrow for the project:

1. Review the milestone breakdown
2. Confirm the total amount
3. Fund escrow from your connected wallet

Escrow protects both you and your client by holding funds until milestones are approved.

## Step 4: Deliver and get paid

As you complete milestones:

1. Mark the milestone as complete
2. Submit deliverables for review
3. The client approves the milestone
4. Funds are released from escrow
5. An invoice is generated automatically

> Business Tip: Always get milestone approval in writing. The client portal makes this easy.

## Step 5: Review and iterate

After completing the project:

- Review the project analytics
- Check your payment history
- Collect feedback from your client
- Use insights to improve your next project
