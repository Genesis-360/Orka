## The Problem Isn't Your Team.

It's Your Workflow.

Every service business starts with the same goal:

> Deliver great work.

But as your business grows, something unexpected happens.

You don't spend more time creating.

You spend more time managing.

Managing clients.

Managing projects.

Managing files.

Managing invoices.

Managing payments.

Managing updates.

Eventually, running the business becomes more work than delivering the service itself.

<Quote>
**Growth shouldn't mean spending more time on administration.**
</Quote>

---

## Common Misconceptions

<Accordion>

### Is Orka only a project management tool?

No.

Project management is only one part of a much larger connected workflow.

---

### Is Orka just an AI writing tool?

No.

AI supports your workflow, but Orka is designed to manage your entire business operation.

---

### Can I still use my existing tools?

Absolutely.

Many teams start by using Orka alongside their existing workflow and gradually centralize more of their operations over time.
