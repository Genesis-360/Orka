Orka isn't just another project management tool. It's the operating system for modern service businesses.

## The problem with traditional tools

Most service businesses use a patchwork of tools:

- One tool for proposals
- Another for contracts
- A separate invoicing system
- A different payment platform
- Spreadsheets for tracking everything

This creates friction, wastes time, and makes it hard to get a clear picture of your business.

## How Orka is different

Orka combines everything into one platform:

| Feature | Traditional Tools | Orka |
|---------|------------------|------|
| Proposals | Separate tool | Built-in with AI |
| Contracts | Manual drafting | AI-generated |
| Projects | Basic tracking | Milestones + escrow |
| Invoicing | Manual creation | Auto-generated |
| Payments | Multiple platforms | Stellar-based |
| Team | Limited collaboration | Full team management |

> Business Tip: Calculate how much time you spend switching between tools each week. Orka eliminates that overhead entirely.

## Built for service businesses

Orka was designed specifically for:

- Freelancers who need to get paid faster
- Agencies managing multiple clients and projects
- Consultants juggling proposals and deliverables
- Studios collaborating on creative work
- Creative teams who want to focus on their craft

## The Orka advantage

AI-powered workflows — Generate proposals, contracts, and invoices in seconds with AI assistance.

Stellar-based payments — Fast, low-cost cross-border payments on the Stellar network.

Escrow protection — Milestone-based escrow keeps both you and your clients safe.

Client portal — Give clients a shared view of projects, invoices, and progress.
