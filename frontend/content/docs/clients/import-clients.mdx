Import multiple clients at once from a CSV file or other tools.

## How to import clients

1. Go to Clients
2. Click Import
3. Choose your source (CSV, Google Contacts, etc.)
4. Map the columns to Orka fields
5. Review and confirm

## CSV format

Your CSV should include these columns:

- Name (required)
- Email
- Company
- Phone

## What happens after import

- Clients are added to your workspace
- Duplicate emails are skipped
- You can edit details after import

> Business Tip: Import clients from your existing tools to save time. You can always edit their profiles later.
