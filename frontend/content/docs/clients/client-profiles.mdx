View and manage all your client information from one place.

## What's in a client profile

- Contact details — Name, email, phone, company
- Projects — All projects linked to this client
- Invoices — Outstanding and paid invoices
- Activity — History of interactions and updates

## How to access a client profile

1. Go to Clients in the sidebar
2. Click on a client name
3. View their full profile

## Editing client details

Click the Edit button on any client profile to update their information.

> Business Tip: Keep client profiles up to date. Accurate information helps you communicate professionally and send invoices to the right person.
