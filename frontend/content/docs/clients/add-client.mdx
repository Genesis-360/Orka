Adding clients to Orka lets you track projects, invoices, and communication in one place.

## How to add a client

1. Click **Clients** in the sidebar
2. Click **Add Client**
3. Fill in the client details
4. Click **Save**

## Client information

Capture the essentials:

- **Name** — Company or individual name
- **Email** — Primary contact email
- **Phone** — Optional contact number
- **Company** — Business name (if applicable)
- **Address** — Billing address
- **Currency** — Preferred payment currency

> **Business Tip:** Always add a primary contact email. This is used for sending invoices and project updates.

## Client portal access

When you add a client, you can optionally enable the client portal. This gives them:

- A shared view of project progress
- Access to invoices and payment history
- Ability to approve milestones
- Secure file sharing

> **Business Tip:** Enable the client portal for transparency. Clients appreciate seeing real-time project progress.
