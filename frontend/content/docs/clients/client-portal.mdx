Give your clients a professional portal to view projects, invoices, and deliverables.

## What clients can see

- Projects — Progress, milestones, and deliverables
- Invoices — Outstanding and paid invoices
- Files — Shared documents and assets
- Timeline — Project activity and updates

## How to enable the client portal

1. Go to Workspace Settings
2. Click Client Portal
3. Toggle Enable Client Portal
4. Customize the branding (optional)

## Sharing the portal with clients

Each client gets a unique link. Share it via email or directly from Orka.

> Business Tip: A client portal reduces back-and-forth emails and builds trust through transparency.
