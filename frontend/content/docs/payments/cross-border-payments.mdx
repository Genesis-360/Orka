Send and receive payments from anywhere in the world with low fees.

## Why cross-border matters

- Work with clients globally
- Accept payments in multiple currencies
- Avoid expensive bank transfer fees

## How it works in Orka

1. Client pays in their currency
2. Stellar converts to your preferred currency
3. Funds arrive in your wallet

## Supported currencies

- USD, EUR, GBP, and 30+ currencies
- Real-time conversion rates
- Low transaction fees

> Business Tip: Set your workspace currency to match where most of your clients are based. It reduces conversion fees.
