View and manage all your payment transactions in one place.

## What you can see

- All incoming payments
- All outgoing payments
- Escrow transactions
- Refund history

## How to access payment history

1. Go to **Payments** in the sidebar
2. Click **History**
3. Filter by date, client, or status

## Exporting records

- Export as CSV for accounting
- Download PDF invoices
- Sync with accounting tools

> **Business Tip:** Review your payment history monthly. It helps you spot trends and forecast cash flow.
