Orka generates invoices automatically when milestones are approved, or you can create them manually.

## Automatic invoicing

When a client approves a milestone:

1. Escrow funds are released
2. An invoice is generated automatically
3. The client receives the invoice via email
4. Payment is recorded in your history

> Business Tip: Automatic invoicing saves hours of admin work each week. Let Orka handle the paperwork.

## Manual invoicing

To create an invoice manually:

1. Click Payments in the sidebar
2. Click Create Invoice
3. Select the client
4. Add line items
5. Send the invoice

## Invoice details

- Client — Who the invoice is for
- Line Items — Services or deliverables
- Amount — Total amount due
- Due Date — Payment deadline
- Notes — Additional terms or information

## Payment methods

Clients can pay via:

- Stellar wallet transfer
- Bank transfer (cross-border)
- Credit card (where available)

> Business Tip: Offer multiple payment options. The easier it is to pay, the faster you get paid.
