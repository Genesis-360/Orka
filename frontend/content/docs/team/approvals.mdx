Create approval workflows to ensure quality before work is marked complete.

## How approvals work

1. Team member submits milestone
2. Approver reviews deliverables
3. Approve or request changes
4. Payment releases on approval

## Setting up approvals

1. Go to **Workspace Settings**
2. Click **Approvals**
3. Choose approval mode:
   - **Single approval** — One person approves
   - **Multi-approval** — Multiple people must approve

## Approval rules

- Assign approvers per project
- Set approval deadlines
- Auto-approve after timeout (optional)

> **Business Tip:** Approval workflows prevent scope creep and ensure quality. They protect both you and your clients.
