Monitor what your team is doing across the workspace.

## What's tracked

- Project changes
- Client updates
- Invoice activity
- Settings changes

## How to view activity

1. Go to Team → Activity
2. Filter by date, member, or type
3. Click on any entry for details

## Activity feed

- Real-time updates
- Sortable by date
- Searchable by keyword

> Business Tip: Review activity weekly to stay aware of workspace changes and catch issues early.
