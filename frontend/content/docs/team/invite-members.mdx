Build your team in Orka to collaborate on projects, share workloads, and scale your business.

## How to invite members

1. Go to **Team** in the sidebar
2. Click **Invite Members**
3. Enter email addresses
4. Assign roles
5. Send invitations

## Team roles

| Role | What they can do |
|------|-----------------|
| **Admin** | Full access to settings, billing, and team management |
| **Member** | Manage projects, clients, invoices, and view reports |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access to projects and activity |

> **Business Tip:** Start with Admin for yourself and Member for collaborators. You can adjust roles as your team grows.

## What team members see

Once invited, team members can:

- View and manage assigned projects
- Create and edit proposals and contracts
- Track milestones and deliverables
- Communicate with clients
- View team activity

## Managing your team

As an admin, you can:

- Change member roles
- Remove members
- View activity logs
- Set up approval workflows

> **Business Tip:** Set up approval workflows for milestone sign-offs. This ensures quality as your team scales.
