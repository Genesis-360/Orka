Understand the different roles and what each can access.

## Role types

- **Admin** — Full access to everything
- **Member** — Manage projects and clients
- **Viewer** — Read-only access

## What each role can do

| Action | Admin | Member | Viewer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Manage settings | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Create projects | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Send invoices | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| View reports | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Manage team | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |

## How to assign roles

1. Go to **Team** → **Members**
2. Click on a member
3. Select their role

> **Business Tip:** Start with Admin and Member roles. Add Viewer for clients who need project visibility.
